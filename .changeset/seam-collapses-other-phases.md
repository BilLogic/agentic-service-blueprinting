Opening a scenario through the shared seam expands that scenario's phase and collapses the phases the sidebar had expanded on the reader's behalf. Navigating by palette, breadcrumb or cover action into phase B after phase A now leaves B open and A closed, instead of accumulating every phase visited in a session. A phase the reader expanded by hand keeps their claim and survives navigation elsewhere; a phase they collapsed by hand is expanded again, as navigation's, when they next open a scenario inside it.
