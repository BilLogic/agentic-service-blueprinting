Opening a scenario through the shared seam collapses the phases the sidebar expanded on the reader's behalf. Navigating by palette, breadcrumb or cover action into phase B after phase A now leaves B open and A closed, instead of accumulating every phase visited in a session. A phase the reader opened or closed by hand becomes theirs from that moment and stays as they left it across navigation.
