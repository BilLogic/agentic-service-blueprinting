The badge-and-tag vocabulary walk survives an entry that disappears between
being listed and being stated, and a new assertion counts what the walk came
back with and names the directories that must be in it. A deployment gains a
guard that goes red for vocabulary and not for a build artefact removed
mid-run, and one that cannot quietly narrow to a handful of directories — the
sampling gap where a retired name lands outside `components/` and nothing
looks.
