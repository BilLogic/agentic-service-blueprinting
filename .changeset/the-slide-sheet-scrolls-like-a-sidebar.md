The slide sheet now takes the height its divider gives it, as the sidebar does. The divider used to set only a maximum, so dragging past the tallest card moved nothing. A card taller than the sheet now scrolls inside itself instead of cutting off its images and caption. The sideways scroll bar is gone: a trackpad swipes across, and a mouse wheel scrolls the strip sideways once the card under the pointer has nowhere left to scroll.
