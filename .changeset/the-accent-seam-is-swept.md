The brand-accent seam is swept around the hue wheel rather than measured at
the one accent this template ships. A deployment that layers its own theme —
a non-zero chroma, its own accent — gains proof that `semantic.css`'s
derivations hold for the accent it chose: the ink flip on the filled control,
the focus ring against a canvas tinted with the same brand, and the hairline
that edges the fill. The flip's worst case was stated in prose beside the
arithmetic and held by nothing; it is measured now, and it is 3.44:1 at the
threshold.
