# sb-whatif.svg — a proposed change → its consequences, before commitment

## Job

Answers: *what does sb:whatif do for me?* Play the change out on the
blueprint before anyone commits resources. Textbook grounding: "what if…"
scenarios and "if… then" decisions worked through on the blueprint with
all stakeholders.

## Template deltas only

- Title: **"sb:whatif — play it out before you build it"**.
- LEFT tab: `A PROPOSED CHANGE`. Contents: mini-grid quote (~260×150,
  dimmed) with ONE cell carrying the emphasis treatment
  (`#d9e4ea`/`#9aadbe`) and an amber edge-label pill riding on it:
  "what if we drop the sign-up call?". Downstream of it, 4–5 cells get a
  soft halo (stroke `#d4b483` @1.2, no fill change) connected by the
  existing trigger-arrow idiom — the ripple, drawn with anatomy's own
  vocabulary.
- Skill chip: **sb:whatif** · `.sub` "play it out first".
- Guardrail CAPS: `TRACED ON A COPY — THE LIVE BLUEPRINT IS NEVER TOUCHED`.
- RIGHT tab: `THE CONSEQUENCES, FIRST`. Contents: one change-request card
  (white, rx=14, ~300×120) laid out like a real summary:
  - `.title` "change request — drop the sign-up call"
  - three `.chip` lines at 26px pitch: "7 cells affected across 3 lanes" ·
    "demand lands on chat support" · "2 assumptions break — flagged for
    evidence"
  - footer row inside the card, 22h: `accept ▸ promote via sb:map ▸
    re-import` (`.chip mono dim`)
- Caption: "work through the if–then decisions on the blueprint before
  anyone commits resources"

## Consistency notes

- The ripple uses anatomy's trigger arrows + a halo — no new visual
  vocabulary for "impact".
- Numbers on the card are illustrative but must stay plausible for the
  drawn ripple (7 affected ↔ ~5 haloed + 2 off-grid implied is fine;
  don't let text and drawing contradict).
- The dashed=copy rule: RESOLVED (Bill 2026-08-08) — the left panel's
  border IS dashed `4 3`; the "traced on a copy" claim gets its visual
  echo. The one sanctioned panel-construction difference among the four
  skill figures.
